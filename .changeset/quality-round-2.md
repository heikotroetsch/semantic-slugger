Quality round 2: more readable IDs. ID output changed.

- The word that tells sibling titles apart (year, North/South, Phase N, week) is kept more often (`weath-warn-north`, `electric-supp-26`).
- Fewer 2 to 4 letter stubs; two whole words win over three or four cuts (`rekordhitze-juli`, `coral-reef-recov`).
- Real letters instead of random codes: a cut grows into its own word, a weekday or month into its full name (`employe-handbook`, `open-house-satur`); numbers stay at the end (`release-note-v23`).
- Negations and privative heads are kept (`nobake-pean-bars`, `glutenfr-cookies`); short compound heads (`zeit`, `fest`) are no longer shown alone.
- Cuts and fills never spell a common other word (`star`, `stock`, `tea`, `schul`).
- More frame and filler words (`beginner`, `guidelines`, `near`, `vs`, `Überblick`) give way to the topic.
