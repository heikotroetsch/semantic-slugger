Quality round 3: more readable IDs. ID output changed.

- The main word of the title is kept more often (`bahnstrei-montag`, `laptop-stan-dres`, `niedersc-nord-de`).
- The word that tells sibling titles apart (phase, side, region) is kept more often (`trial-compou-ph1`, `kniegelenk-links`, `holid-camp-north`).
- A fill that shows a real word replaces stubs and random codes, in title order (`weekend-stockhol`, `hallowee-costume`, `team-lunch-frida`, `todolisten-schre`, `impfplan-kin-6yr`).
- Common multi-word terms stay one unit and use their short form (`rfq-office-furni`, `sow-data-migrati`, `coc-engl-edition`, `castiron-skillet`, `deeplearn-segmen`).
- Fewer cuts that spell another word (`chick`, `liver`, `custom`, `veg`, `uml`) and no generic compound heads alone (`vertrag`, `reinigung`).
- `#812` shows `812` or `no812` outside German titles.
